What remains outside the claim

Non-exercise subset of the previously compiler-audited release. Assigned exercise solutions are withheld. Further non-exercise formalization and interaction updates are in progress.

These declarations check ordinary Mathlib category theory. They do not formalize the whole lecture notes, Synthetic Category Theory, infinity-category theory, HoTT or the existence of set universes.

Checked scope: identity laws; uniqueness of inverses; and an explicit finite equivalence that is not surjective on objects. The general Yoneda lemma, adjunction correspondence and equivalence criterion are not proved by these modules.

Mathematical explanations and proof sketches are independently authored educational metadata, distinct from compiler output. No mathematical novelty is claimed.
