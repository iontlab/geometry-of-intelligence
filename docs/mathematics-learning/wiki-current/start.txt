====== Synthetic Category Theory ======

**[Draft] · A lecture-note learning project within [[:mathematics_learning:higher_category_theory:start|Higher Category Theory]].**

How much can we learn about an object by studying the maps into and out of it? This project develops that question from ordinary categories to the Yoneda lemma, then uses adjunctions and equivalences to compare mathematical constructions.

**Binding primary source:** //Lecture Notes: Synthetic Category Theory//, Lyne Moser (Universität Hamburg) and Tashi Walde (Universität Regensburg), supplied 13-page installment. [[:mathematics_learning:higher_category_theory:synthetic_category_theory:sources|Sources, official author links, editions and conventions]] identifies all three references and their different roles.

**Primary reading:** The complete original Moser–Walde manuscript is published as complete native text and typeset mathematics across the eight chapters below, preserving all original proofs, diagrams and exercise markers. {{:mathematics_learning:synthetic_category_theory:original:moser-walde-13-pages.pdf|Complete original PDF (selectable text)}}.
