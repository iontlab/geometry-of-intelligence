===== Scope and conventions =====

These are ordinary Mathlib categories. Lean's ''f ≫ g'' means the source's \(g\circ f\). Object and hom universes are independently parameterized; they do not prove the existence of the primary's set universes. The finite ''walkingEquivalence'' includes a triangle law because Mathlib bundles an adjoint equivalence, a stronger data requirement than primary Definition 3.4.

The inverse-uniqueness source code exchanges the names of the left and right inverse relative to the written lesson. Its conclusion is the same uniqueness result. The finite example proves essential-surjectivity witnesses and failure of literal surjectivity directly. It does not use an unproved general criterion.

**Not formalized here:** HoTT, univalence, Synthetic Category Theory, infinity-categories, set-universe existence, the general Yoneda lemma, the adjunction hom-set correspondence, and Theorem 3.7's general equivalence criterion. Assigned exercise solutions are outside the publication scope. Other non-exercise examples remain written mathematics pending the expanded verified release. Complete explanatory coverage does not mean complete Lean coverage.
