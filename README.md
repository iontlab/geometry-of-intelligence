# iLab — Geometry of Intelligence

[iLab’s canonical research wiki](https://iontlab.org/) is the human-readable home for research framing, ontology, definitions, mathematical development, conjectures, reading notes, status, and roadmap.

This public repository is the wiki’s technical / artifact companion. It holds source-controlled formalization artifacts and their technical documentation.

## Current artifacts

- [RQ-001 Phase I — Lean Formalization](formalization/rq001/README.md): four independently developed modules, pinned Lean/Mathlib project, verification evidence, and a compiler-derived declaration graph.
- [Interactive formalization graph](https://iontlab.org/formalization/rq001/).

Selected mathematical results from RQ-001 Phase I are Lean-verified. This is a partial formalization of the mathematical core, not a formalization of RQ-001 as a whole. RQ-001 remains open and its research note remains [Draft].

The wiki remains the canonical research space. This repository does not duplicate the wiki, and no automatic synchronization is established.
